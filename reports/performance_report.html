Label-based performance monitoring

Datasets and method

Reference: baseline test set. Batches: control (no change), data_drift (inflation and unemployment) and concept_drift (same inputs, higher risk for clients with high payment burden). Calibration gap = observed default rate - mean predicted risk; alert threshold: 1%.

Interpretation

  • reference: observed default 8.07% vs predicted 8.10% (gap -0.03 p.p.): calibrated. ROC-AUC 0.732.
  • control: observed default 8.07% vs predicted 8.06% (gap +0.01 p.p.): calibrated. ROC-AUC 0.736.
  • data_drift: observed default 9.59% vs predicted 8.18% (gap +1.42 p.p.): the model UNDERESTIMATES risk. ROC-AUC 0.711.
  • concept_drift: observed default 9.69% vs predicted 8.06% (gap +1.63 p.p.): the model UNDERESTIMATES risk. ROC-AUC 0.695.

Limitations

  • Labels are simulated; in production they arrive months after the decision.
  • Segments are known here because the scenario was designed; in practice they are investigated after a global alert.
  • The unemployed default rate (29.4%) comes from only 17 reference clients.
